Privacy Policy — K Downloader

Last updated: 14 October 2026

K Downloader is a download manager: it saves files from links you give it. This page explains exactly what the app does and does not send anywhere.

Your downloads stay on your phone

When you start a download, the app connects directly to the website the link points to and saves the file in Download/K Downloader on your device. The request goes to that website only — the same way a browser would — and may carry what that site needs to serve the file, such as a browser user-agent and, for downloads caught in the built-in browser, that site's cookies and referring page. Your links, file names, files and download history are never uploaded to us or shared with anyone. The download list, settings, scheduled downloads and speed limits live only on your phone.

The built-in browser

Browsing history, bookmarks, open tabs and website cookies are stored on your device only. You can erase them at any time in Settings → Clear browsing data.

The clipboard

The app looks at the clipboard only when you tap + to add a download, to offer a link you just copied. It is never read in the background, and you can turn this off in Settings.

What the app does send

  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) — when the app stops unexpectedly, a technical report is sent so the fault can be fixed. It contains the error, the device model and the Android version.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) — such as how often the app is opened and which screens are used. Links, file names and browsing history are never included.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob) — the app shows ads, and the ads SDK collects a device advertising identifier and approximate, IP-derived location to select and measure them. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before any ad is requested.

What the app never collects

  • Your name, email address, phone number or contacts
  • Your precise location
  • The links you download, the files, their names or their contents
  • Your browsing history — it stays on the device
  • Any account — K Downloader has no sign-in

Permissions and why

  • Internet and network state — to download files, and to pause downloads when you chose "Wi-Fi only" and the phone is on mobile data.
  • Notifications — to show download progress and the Pause button while files download in the background.
  • Foreground service (data sync) — so downloads you started keep running when you leave the app.
  • Run at startup — only to re-arm downloads you scheduled for later after the phone restarts.
  • Storage — on Android 9 and older only, to save files in the Download folder. Newer Android versions need no storage permission.

Children

K Downloader is not directed at children under 13.

Third-party services

The services above have their own policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Your choices

You can reset or delete the advertising identifier in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. You can clear browsing data in the app's Settings at any time. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your device except the files you downloaded, which stay in your Download folder until you delete them.
